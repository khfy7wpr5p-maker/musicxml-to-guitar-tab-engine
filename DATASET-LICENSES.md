# Dataset and source-material licenses

No raw training dataset is distributed by this repository. Dataset rights do
not pass through the repository's software license.

The retained GuitarSet-derived research model was trained from GuitarSet
1.1.0, DOI [`10.5281/zenodo.3371780`](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3371780),
released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Required
attribution: Qingyang Xi, Rachel M. Bittner, Johan Pauwels, Xuzhou Ye, and
Juan Pablo Bello, “GuitarSet: A Dataset for Guitar Transcription,” ISMIR 2018.

Anyone obtaining or reusing source data must obtain it from its authoritative
source, preserve attribution and notices, and independently confirm that the
intended use complies with the source terms. Model licensing does not erase
rights that may subsist in training material.
