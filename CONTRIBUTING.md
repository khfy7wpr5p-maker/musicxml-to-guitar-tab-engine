# Contributing

Before a contribution can be merged, the contributor must agree to
`CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md` using the repository's designated written
record. Signed-off commits alone do not replace that agreement.

Contributors must have the right to submit their work, identify third-party
material, preserve upstream notices, and avoid confidential, personal, or
uncleared training data. Contributions must pass the repository's required
tests and license-policy checks. Acceptance is at the project owner's
discretion.
